About Us

We are a Swedish consultancy at the intersection of industrial domain expertise and artificial intelligence. Unlike generic AI companies, our strength lies in combining deep scientific and engineering knowledge with practical AI capabilities, applied specifically to the realities of industrial environments. Our Expertise

With a foundation in materials science, computational modelling and mathematical methods, combined with a background in finance and business strategy, we bridge two worlds that rarely meet: rigorous scientific thinking and commercial decision-making. That combination is rare. And for companies in energy, manufacturing and process industries, it changes what's possible.

Our Approach

We work hands-on and collaboratively with industrial companies - assessing data infrastructure, building internal AI capabilities, and translating complex AI concepts into strategies your organisation can actually execute. We don't deliver reports and disappear. We work alongside your teams to ensure that what we recommend is understood, owned and implemented.

Operating from Sweden, we serve industrial companies across the Nordics and internationally. Services delivered in English or Swedish.
