Data & AI Readiness Audit

Many industrial AI initiatives fail not because the algorithms are weak, but because the data and foundations are not ready. Our audit gives you a fact-based view of where you stand today — and where AI can realistically create value with the data you have.

We offer three levels of engagement depending on how deep you need to go. All are designed specifically for metals, mining and process industries — not as generic IT assessments.

Tier 1 — Entry Point

AI Readiness Score

Same day

A structured 90-minute conversation covering six dimensions of AI readiness. No data access required. You leave with a concrete score, clear gaps identified, and a prioritised roadmap for what to address first.

  • 60-minute structured interview
  • Score across 6 dimensions (0–100)
  • 3-page report with gap analysis
  • Prioritised action plan
  • 30-minute follow-up Q&A
  • No data access required
Tier 2 — Deeper Analysis

Data Sample Analysis

1 week

Everything in Tier 1, plus a structured review of sample datasets you provide. We assess completeness, consistency, and suitability for your target AI use cases — giving you a technically grounded picture before committing to larger investments.

  • Everything in Tier 1
  • Review of 2–3 sample datasets
  • Data quality report (completeness, consistency, outliers)
  • Assessment of data suitability for target AI use cases
  • Specific technical recommendations
Tier 3 — Full Engagement

Full Data Audit

4–6 weeks

A comprehensive engagement covering your full data landscape — from discovery and data quality analysis to use-case mapping and a 12-month implementation roadmap. Designed for organisations ready to commit seriously to AI.

  • Full data landscape mapping across all systems
  • Deep data quality assessment (historian, SCADA, MES, LIMS)
  • AI use-case portfolio with prioritisation matrix
  • 90-day action plan + 12-month roadmap
  • Executive presentation to leadership
  • Governance & operating model recommendations
